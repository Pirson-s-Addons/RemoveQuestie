## What it does

**Remove Questie** adds a checkbox next to every quest in your quest log and four buttons to clean it up, so you can drop a whole batch of quests at once instead of abandoning them one by one.

## Features

- A **checkbox** next to each quest to mark it.
- **Select All** / **Unselect All** buttons.
- **Abandon Selected** abandons only the ticked quests; **Abandon All** abandons every quest in the log. Both ask for confirmation first.
- **Select Zone** (WoW Forever): pick a zone from the list to tick all its quests, then press **Abandon Selected**.
- Each abandoned quest is listed in the chat.
- Works in the classic quest log (TBC Anniversary, Mists) and in the world map quest log (WoW Forever). Translated into 20 languages.

## Usage

- Open the quest log (`L`), tick the quests you want to drop and press **Abandon Selected**, or press **Abandon All**.
- `/rq compact` (WoW Forever) toggles smaller buttons and text, for small maps like Leatrix Maps.

## Installation

1. Download the zip from the [latest release](https://github.com/Pirson-s-Addons/RemoveQuestie/releases/latest) or from [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/remove-questie).
2. Extract the `RemoveQuestie` folder into the AddOns folder of your game version:
   - TBC Anniversary: `World of Warcraft/_anniversary_/Interface/AddOns/`
   - Classic (Mists): `World of Warcraft/_classic_/Interface/AddOns/`
   - WoW Forever: `World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/`
3. Restart WoW and enable the addon.

## Notes

Both abandon buttons show a confirmation window first (Abandon Selected tells you how many quests it will drop); nothing is abandoned until you press **Yes**. Selections are not saved between sessions. Despite the name, it does not need or touch the Questie addon.
